# Solutions for D06-T002

## Exercise 1

`/v1/customers`, `/v1/customers/{customer_id}`, `/v1/customers/{customer_id}/subscriptions`, `POST` create subscription, `POST /subscriptions/{id}/pauses` and `/resumes` as sub-resources (or `PATCH` status with state machine). Avoid `/pauseSubscription`.

## Exercise 2

Pick no trailing slash; redirect 308 from `/invoices/` to `/invoices` or reject consistently. Update CDN cache keys and integration tests; mixed rules double traffic and split metrics.

## Exercise 3

Nested when every user belongs to one account and auth always scopes by account in path; flat `/users/{id}` when users span accounts and JWT carries `account_id` claim validated server-side. B2B often prefers `/accounts/{account_id}/users` for WAF and audit logs.
