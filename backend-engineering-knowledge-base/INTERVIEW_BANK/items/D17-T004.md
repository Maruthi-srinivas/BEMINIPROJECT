# D17-T004 interview items

Answers: [section K](../16_SYSTEM_DESIGN_PROBLEMS/notes/d17-t004-notification-service.md).

1–5 Beginner: async notify, queue, templates, channels, idempotency.
6–10 Intermediate: priority, DLQ, fan-out, provider limits, opt-out.
11–15 Advanced: ordering, exactly-once, personalization, cost, archiving.
16–20 Scenarios: Black Friday, duplicate push, wrong locale, SMS throttle, GDPR delete.
21–23 Follow-ups: email system, push tokens, metrics.
