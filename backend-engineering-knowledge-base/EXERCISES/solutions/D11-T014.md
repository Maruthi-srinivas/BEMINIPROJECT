# Solutions — D11-T014

## Exercise 1

Redis Pub/Sub delivers a message to subscribers that are connected at that moment. It does not store the message. A subscriber that is down misses it. There is no acknowledgment. It fits ephemeral signals such as 'invalidate this key' to live processes. It does not fit a job queue.

The failure enters when a step is skipped or repeated without an invariant: using Pub/Sub as a job queue and losing every message published during a deploy.

Mark that step on the diagram. If you cannot point to a step, the explanation is still a definition, not a mechanism.
