# Solutions for D04-T008

## Exercise 1

Active: ESTABLISHED → FIN-WAIT-1 → FIN-WAIT-2 → TIME-WAIT → CLOSED after 2MSL. Passive: ESTABLISHED → CLOSE-WAIT until app closes → LAST-ACK → CLOSED.

## Exercise 2

CLOSE-WAIT means local side has not called close after peer FIN—fix connection pool return/close in application code.
