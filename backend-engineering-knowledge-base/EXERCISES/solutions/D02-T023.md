# Solutions — D02-T023

## Exercise 1

Bit manipulation uses bitwise AND, OR, XOR, shifts, and masks to represent sets and flags in a word. XOR is its own inverse, so a^a is 0 and a^0 is a. Shifts move bits and are not arithmetic multiply once the sign bit or a discarded bit matters. A mask isolates a field.

The failure enters when a step is skipped or repeated without an invariant: signed right shift, off-by-one mask width, or treating a bitset as a bigint across languages with different word sizes.

Mark that step on the diagram. If you cannot point to a step, the explanation is still a definition, not a mechanism.
