Open this only after attempting the exercises in the topic note.

## Exercise 1

## Exercise 2

Start from the user-visible SLO window, then compare error rate and saturation metrics, then isolate canary versus stable, then inspect one slow trace or profile, then check the dependency named in the scenario. Roll back if the change correlates with the spike.
