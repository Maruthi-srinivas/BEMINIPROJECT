# Solutions — D09-T013

## Exercise 1

Streaming replication ships WAL records to a standby that replays them, producing a physical copy. Logical replication ships row changes from a publication to a subscription, which can be a subset of tables and can land on a different major version or schema shape. Physical replicas are for failover and read scaling of the whole cluster. Logical replication is for selective copy and upgrades. Both lag.

The failure enters when a step is skipped or repeated without an invariant: promoting a replica that has not received the latest commit, or a logical subscriber stuck on one bad row.

Mark that step on the diagram. If you cannot point to a step, the explanation is still a definition, not a mechanism.

Choose Streaming and logical replication when you need a copy of the data on another node.

Avoid it when you treat a lagged replica as proof of a durable commit on the primary.
