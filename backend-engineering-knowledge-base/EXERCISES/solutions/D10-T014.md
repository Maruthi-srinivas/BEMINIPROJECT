# Solutions — D10-T014

## Exercise 1

Eventual consistency means that if writes stop, replicas converge to the same value. It does not say when, and it does not say what a read returns before that. Conflicts need a rule: last write wins, a merge function, or an application repair. Clients observe stale reads, and sometimes two different values. It is a good trade for high availability during a partition if the data can merge.

The failure enters when a step is skipped or repeated without an invariant: last-write-wins dropping a slower but more important update, such as a stock decrement.

Mark that step on the diagram. If you cannot point to a step, the explanation is still a definition, not a mechanism.

## Exercise 3

Choose Eventual consistency when the data has a merge rule and availability beats freshness.

Avoid it when the operation is a decrement or a unique constraint you cannot reconcile after the fact.

A good answer names the metric that would change your mind: replica divergence time and lost-update count.
