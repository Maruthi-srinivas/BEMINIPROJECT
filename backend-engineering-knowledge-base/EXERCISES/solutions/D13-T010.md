# Solutions — D13-T010

## Exercise 1

The producer-consumer pattern separates work creation from work execution through a queue. Producers enqueue. Consumers dequeue. The queue is the synchronization point. An unbounded queue hides a slow consumer until memory dies. Closing the queue is a signal that no more work is coming, and consumers must exit after draining.

The failure enters when a step is skipped or repeated without an invariant: an unbounded queue that grows until the process is killed, or a consumer that exits and leaves the queue unowned.

Mark that step on the diagram. If you cannot point to a step, the explanation is still a definition, not a mechanism.
