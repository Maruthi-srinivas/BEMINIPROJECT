# Solutions — D10-T009

## Exercise 1

A TTL expires an item after a duration. The store deletes it lazily or with a background process. Expiration is not a transaction. A read might still see an item around its deadline, and a delete might lag. TTL is for caches, sessions, and ephemeral events, not for legal retention. If the expiry must be exact, the application has to check a timestamp too.

The failure enters when a step is skipped or repeated without an invariant: a session that is still accepted after logout because expiry is lazy, or a disk fill because deletes cannot keep up.

Mark that step on the diagram. If you cannot point to a step, the explanation is still a definition, not a mechanism.

Avoid it when you need a precise legal deletion time or an audit trail of the delete.
