# Solutions for D07-T001

Open this only after attempting the exercises.

## Exercise 1

Keep users, trips, payments, and payouts in PostgreSQL with foreign keys and transactions. Store marketing banners or unstructured driver notes as documents if needed. Trips updating payment status and driver availability need one transactional boundary.

## Exercise 2

Reject Cassandra for 500 static rows: ops overhead and CQL modeling cost exceed benefit. PostgreSQL with a single table and cache is simpler; Cassandra wins on huge write-heavy time series, not config.

## Exercise 3

Backfill orders into relational tables nightly with checksum counts; dual-write new orders to both; compare totals; freeze Mongo writes; switch readers to SQL views; decommission after retention window.
